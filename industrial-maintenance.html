Industrial Maintenance

Service Overview

Gonzo Engineering provides industrial maintenance, repairs, and modification services, as well as comprehensive steel fabrication, to support the safe operation of plant, machinery, and facilities, including in the food industry.

Our expertise spans both light steel fabrication, such as handrails, stairs, and domestic applications, and structural steel fabrication.

Our team works closely with customers to identify requirements, implement practical solutions, and minimize disruption to operations.

From planned maintenance to unexpected breakdowns, we focus on getting equipment back into service safely and efficiently.

Scope and Response Arrangements

We deliver comprehensive planned and reactive maintenance, equipment repairs, and custom modifications across industrial, commercial, and domestic sectors.

Our capabilities span heavy structural steel, structural and light stairs, handrails, and balustrades, alongside specialized food-industry fabrications including conveyors, modifications, tables, and benches.

Working across mild steel, stainless steel, aluminum, brass, and copper, we handle both workshop builds and on-site installations.

We also support scheduled plant shutdowns, operational upgrades, and urgent equipment breakdowns.
