About Gonzo Engineering

Gonzo Engineering Pty Ltd is a proudly Australian-owned metal fabrication company with over 30 years of experience in industrial, commercial and residential fabrication and installation.

Founded by Jorge Gonzalez, the company has developed extensive expertise in metalwork and structural steel, serving the construction and food manufacturing industries.

Gonzo Engineering has delivered architectural metalwork projects for internationally recognised brands, including Louis Vuitton, Tiffany & Co. and H&M.

Our reputation is built on the quality of our workmanship and on feedback from customers who value the practical knowledge and support we provide.
